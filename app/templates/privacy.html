Privacy Policy

How MJ WebTech Pvt. Ltd. handles the information you share with us.

Last updated: August 2026

MJ WebTech Pvt. Ltd. (“we”, “us”) operates mjwebtech.in. This policy describes the personal data we collect when you use our website, contact us, create an account, or apply for a role.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: name, email, company, phone, subject, and message.
  • Accounts: name, email, and a hashed password. We verify email with a one-time code.
  • Job applications: name, email, phone, role, experience, cover letter, and resume file.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic request metadata used to protect the site from abuse.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To create and secure your account.
  • To evaluate job applications and contact candidates.
  • To prevent spam, fraud, and unauthorized access.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with infrastructure and email providers needed to run this website (for example hosting and transactional email), or when required by law.

Retention

Enquiry and application records are kept as long as needed for business follow-up and legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized. You may ask us to correct or delete your data by emailing {{ company_email }}.

Security

Passwords and one-time codes are stored hashed. Resume files are not published on the public website and are available only to authorized administrators.
