About MJ WebTech

Who We Are

Built on Expertise, Driven by Results

MJ WebTech Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated in 2026 with a simple premise: businesses in tier-2 India deserve world-class IT services at accessible prices. Founded in Siwan, Bihar, we began with software development and rapidly expanded across eight service domains as our client base grew.

Today we serve 10+ clients spanning healthcare, finance, retail, logistics, education and government sectors — delivering everything from bespoke applications and cloud migrations to on-demand IT staffing and performance marketing campaigns.

Our 15+ strong team of engineers, consultants, marketers and support specialists operate from our Siwan HQ with remote capabilities across India.
